The Problem

Early reading deserves more than drills.

Research on primary classrooms suggests that reading lessons in India tend to focus heavily on grammar and spelling practice. While these are valuable skills, there's often less structured support for fluency, vocabulary, and comprehension; the foundations that help children actually enjoy and understand what they read.


As Chapter Head of ReadItOut, I've spent time running reading workshops with children and seen how much of a difference the right activity at the right level can make. That experience is what led me to build ReadFlow.

"When children engage with reading at the right level, in the right way, the progress is remarkable."



ReadFlow replaces passive drilling with adaptive, gamified activities, giving every child a personalised path to literacy, benchmarked against the Common European Framework of Reference (CEFR).

How It Works

From assessment to growth.

ReadFlow adapts to each child's strengths and gaps in real time, using a CEFR-aligned framework to keep learning always at the right level.

🧪
Start with an assessment

Every child begins with a placement assessment that identifies their CEFR level, so they start exactly where they need to.

🎮
Learn through 15 activities

Gamified activities across 5 categories. Difficulty adapts automatically as children demonstrate growth or need more support.

📈
Track progress in real time

Weekly progress reports, real-time hints, and teacher/parent dashboards ensure continuous guidance and monitoring.

⚖️
Weighted scoring system

Vocabulary and comprehension receive the highest weighting — the skills that matter most for real-world literacy.

👩‍🏫
Monitoring via dashboardsn\

Teachers can structure lessons to the individual. Parents can monitor progress and reinforce learning at home.

🌍
Globally benchmarked

CEFR alignment means every child's progress is measured against an internationally recognised standard.

Inclusivity

Built for all learners.

Accessibility
Dyslexia-friendly design

Typography, spacing, and colour choices are optimised for children with dyslexia; so every child can engage fully.

Adaptivity
Always the right level

The platform continuously adjusts difficulty based on each child's performance; never too easy, never overwhelming.
