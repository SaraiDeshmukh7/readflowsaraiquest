Features that set us apart

15+ interactive games

Activities covering comprehension, vocabulary, grammar, pronunciation, and spelling.

Instant pronunciation feedback

Real-time speech feedback as children read aloud, so mistakes are caught and corrected in the moment.

Personalised learning

Activities adapt to each child's current reading level: never too easy, never overwhelming.

Progress reports

Scores, personalised feedback, and weekly summaries for every child.

Teacher Mode

Teachers create classrooms, add students, and monitor each child's progress in one dashboard.

Dyslexia-friendly mode

Designed with dyslexic and neurodivergent learners in mind, from typography to spacing.

How it works

Levels follow the CEFR, an international scale from A1 (beginner) to C2 (advanced).

  1. 1

    Placement check

    A short assessment finds each child's starting level.

  2. 2

    Practice at their level

    Games and activities matched to where they are right now.

  3. 3

    Feedback as they go

    Instant pronunciation feedback, scores, and weekly summaries.

  4. 4

    Level up

    80% or more in every skill category unlocks the next level.

It started with a question.

“Are we actually teaching children to read, or just teaching them to pronounce words?”

In our reading workshops, we noticed most lessons focus on spelling, grammar, and pronunciation. ReadFlow puts understanding first, and we're designing it to support dyslexic and neurodivergent learners too.

What the research says.

Aa
VocabularyKnowing what words mean
ComprehensionUnderstanding what you read

These are the strongest markers of reading success, so ReadFlow gives them the most weight while still building pronunciation, spelling, and grammar.

Research by Reed et al. (2016), Oakhill & Cain (2012), and Hjetland et al. (2017) informed our approach.
